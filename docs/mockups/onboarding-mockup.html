Rainbow Timer · Mockup

First run: how to set the timer

The dial is interactive. On a fresh start, a ghost handle sweeps clockwise twice, then the numbers pulse twice. A hint line under the dial stays while no timer is running or paused. The handle shows on hover for good. The how-to lives behind an info button.

How it works

  1. Drag the handle clockwise around the dial.
  2. Let go and the timer starts.
  3. Tap the center to pause or resume.
Tap a number to jump straight to it. Switch hr / min / sec below the dial.
Desktop: hover the dial to see the handle follow your cursor. Touch: the two sweeps teach the gesture.

What changed from the first draft

A · Ghost handle

Plays twice, then stops

A pulsing handle at 12 o'clock and a faint rainbow sweep, two loops (about 6 s). Afterwards nothing moves. The handle comes back on hover only: a preview handle follows the cursor along the rim whenever the dial is idle.

B · Number wave

Right after A, also twice

The numbers 5 to 60 grow one after another, clockwise, two rounds. It starts when A has finished, so only one thing moves at a time.

Hint text

Under the dial, until a timer runs

“Drag the handle clockwise, or tap a number” stays visible as long as no timer is running or paused. While dragging it reads “release to start”, and it hands over to the existing “ends at …” and “paused” captions.

C · Info button

How-to behind “i”

Three steps plus two tips in a small popover. A pink dot on the button stays until it has been opened once.

Where the info button lands

Recommended

In the control pill

  • Sits between the unit switch and fullscreen, where users already look for controls.
  • The popover opens upward from the pill with its arrow on the button.
  • On forced fullscreen (low height) the pill is split: put the info button next to sound at the bottom left.

Top-left corner

  • Mirrors the theme toggle at the top right and keeps the pill short.
  • Further from the dial, so it is easier to miss on a first run.
  • Competes with the title on narrow phones.

Rules

  • Hint animations and the caption only while idle: angle 0, no timer, not paused.
  • The first touch on the dial ends all animations at once.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: static ghost, no pulsing.
  • Popover closes on Esc, outside tap and the close button, and returns focus to the button.

Where it goes in the code

  • Dial: hint prop ('ghost' | 'wave' | 'none') and a hover handle.
  • HubContent: new { kind: 'hint' }.
  • rainbow-timer.tsx: idle caption, info button, popover.
  • Plays once per page load; whether it ever shows again is left open.
